✦ GET TO KNOW QUIZIVIA

A Little About Our Learning World

Quizivia is an interactive trivia platform where curiosity meets friendly competition. It gives learners a place to explore different subjects, challenge their knowledge, and celebrate their progress through quizzes, experience points, missions, and achievements.

How to Begin Your Adventure

  1. Create Your Account. Register using your username and PIN.
  2. Choose a Subject. Explore History, Science, Mathematics, or General Knowledge.
  3. Pick a Difficulty. Challenge yourself with Easy, Medium, or Hard quizzes when available.
  4. Answer the Questions. Read carefully and submit your answers.
  5. Earn Experience. Complete quizzes and qualify for XP rewards.
  6. Build Your Record. Visit your Profile to review your progress, missions, and achievements.

Experience Points and Levels

XP represents your progress as a Quizivia player. Every correct answer and completed challenge brings you closer to your next level.

CATEGORY QUIZ +10 XP per correct answer
DAILY QUIZ 50–100 XP per completion

Daily Quiz rewards depend on your final score. Each player can claim one Daily Quiz reward per day.

Understanding Player Levels

  • Every 100 accumulated XP advances you by one level.
  • Your remaining XP progress appears on your Home page and Profile.
  • Continue earning XP to reach higher levels and player titles.

Daily Missions

Daily Missions give you extra goals to complete. Explore the platform, improve your quiz performance, and earn bonus experience.

  • Quiz Explorer: Complete a category quiz — +10 XP.
  • Sharp Mind: Get at least 5 correct answers in category quizzes — +15 XP.
  • Daily Challenger: Complete your Daily Quiz — +20 XP.

Achievements and Milestones

Achievements celebrate your dedication and progress. Work toward these milestones and collect their rewards.

  • First Steps: Complete your first quiz — +10 XP.
  • Knowledge Seeker: Complete 5 quizzes — +25 XP.
  • Quiz Champion: Reach Level 10 — +100 XP.
  • Consistency Starter: Reach a 3-day streak — +15 XP.
  • Weekly Warrior: Reach a 7-day streak — +35 XP.
  • Knowledge Devotee: Reach a 14-day streak — +75 XP.
  • Quizivia Legend: Reach a 30-day streak — +150 XP.

Climb the Leaderboard

Your effort deserves to be celebrated. The Quizivia Leaderboard lets you compare player XP through Weekly Rankings and All-Time Rankings. Keep learning, earn more experience, and see how far you can climb.
