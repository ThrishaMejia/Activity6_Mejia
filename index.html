✦ ABOUT THE GAME ✦

What is Quizivia?

Quizivia is an interactive trivia quiz website designed to make learning more exciting. Explore different subjects, test your knowledge, earn experience points, and challenge yourself to become a quiz champion!

🎮 How to Play

  1. Create Your Player Account. Register with your username and PIN to start your adventure.
  2. Choose a Category. Explore subjects such as History, Science, Mathematics, and General Knowledge.
  3. Select Your Difficulty. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard, depending on the available quiz options.
  4. Answer the Questions. Read each question carefully and submit your answers.
  5. Check Your Results. View your score, accuracy, and XP earned after completing the quiz.
  6. Track Your Progress. Visit your Profile to see your XP, level, quiz history, missions, and achievements.

⭐ XP and Level System

Experience Points (XP) measure your progress in Quizivia. Earn XP by completing quizzes and qualifying for rewards. The more XP you collect, the higher your player level becomes!

CATEGORY QUIZ +10 XP per correct answer
DAILY QUIZ 50–100 XP per completion

Your Daily Quiz reward depends on your final score. You can complete one Daily Quiz attempt per day.

🏅 How Levels Work

  • Every 100 accumulated XP advances you by one level.
  • Your remaining XP progress is shown on your Profile and Home page.
  • Keep earning XP to unlock higher player levels and titles.

🎯 Daily Missions

Complete special daily objectives to earn bonus XP. Missions encourage you to explore categories, answer questions, and complete your Daily Quiz.

  • Quiz Explorer: Complete a category quiz — +10 XP.
  • Sharp Mind: Get at least 5 correct answers in category quizzes — +15 XP.
  • Daily Challenger: Complete your Daily Quiz — +20 XP.

🏆 Achievements

Achievements recognize your milestones and dedication. Complete quizzes, reach higher levels, and maintain your Daily Quiz streak to qualify for achievement rewards.

  • First Steps: Complete your first quiz — +10 XP.
  • Knowledge Seeker: Complete 5 quizzes — +25 XP.
  • Quiz Champion: Reach Level 10 — +100 XP.
  • Consistency Starter: Reach a 3-day streak — +15 XP.
  • Weekly Warrior: Reach a 7-day streak — +35 XP.
  • Knowledge Devotee: Reach a 14-day streak — +75 XP.
  • Quizivia Legend: Reach a 30-day streak — +150 XP.

👑 Leaderboard

Show off your progress on the Quizivia Leaderboard! Compare your accumulated XP with other players through the All-Time Rankings, or see who earned the most XP during the current week in the Weekly Rankings.

Keep learning, complete challenges, collect XP, and aim for the top!
